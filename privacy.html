1. Overview

InfoAll (“we,” “our” or “the website”) provides travel-related information, news links and destination content. This policy describes how information may be handled when you visit the website, use its features or contact us.

2. Information collected

The information collected depends on which features you enable:

  • Information you provide: If you contact us, you may provide your name, email address, topic and message.
  • Saved stories and preferences: The News page may store bookmarked articles and theme preferences in your browser's local storage. These values are stored on your device by the website and are not, by that feature alone, sent to our server.
  • Technical data: The web host or server may process technical information such as IP address, request time, browser details and error logs as part of operating and protecting the site. Confirm this with your hosting setup.
  • AI or other interactive features: If enabled, information you submit to those features may be sent to the server and relevant service provider to generate a response. Explain the exact providers and data handling here before launch.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to operate and maintain the website, respond to inquiries, troubleshoot errors, protect against abuse, remember your local preferences and provide features you request. We do not claim to collect or use information beyond what your deployed website and connected services actually process.

4. Cookies and browser storage

The News page may use browser local storage to remember saved stories and the light/dark theme. Browser settings can allow you to clear this stored data. Your hosting provider, analytics tools or other integrations may also use cookies or similar technologies; list those tools here if you add them.

5. Third-party services and external links

News articles link to third-party publishers. If the site retrieves news from a news API, the server sends requests to that provider. When you open external sites, their own privacy policies and terms apply. Identify your actual news API, AI provider, hosting provider, analytics service and other integrations here, and link to their policies where appropriate.

6. Security and retention

We recommend limiting access to server logs, keeping API keys on the server in environment variables, using HTTPS in production and collecting only information needed for the stated purpose. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Retention periods depend on your hosting, logging and connected service configurations; add the actual periods before publishing.

7. Your choices and rights

You can clear your browser's local storage to remove locally saved stories and preferences. For information submitted through a contact form or other connected services, contact the website operator to ask about access, correction or deletion. Your rights depend on the laws that apply to you and how the website operates. Add a clear process for privacy requests and the relevant jurisdiction-specific disclosures before launch.

8. Children's privacy

This website is not intended to collect children's personal information unnecessarily. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the site, contact the website operator so the matter can be reviewed.

9. Policy updates

We may update this policy when the website or its data practices change. The updated version should show a revised date. Please review this page periodically.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the Contact page. Replace this template with your real contact details and confirm that the contact process works before publishing.
