A FEW QUICK ANSWERS

Before you set off.

A little clarity before the adventure begins.

Are these guides suitable for first-time travellers?＋

Yes. The guides are written to be approachable, with practical ideas for planning, getting around and making thoughtful choices. Always check current local guidance before you travel.

Can I save guides for later?＋

Yes. Use the bookmark icon on any guide. Saved guides stay in this browser on this device through local storage; clearing site data may remove them.

Does the trip planner book hotels or transport?＋

No. It creates a simple planning outline only. It does not make bookings, check live availability or guarantee prices.

Are prices and opening hours guaranteed to be current?＋

No. Travel conditions can change. Confirm prices, timings, weather, transport schedules and entry requirements with official sources before departure.
